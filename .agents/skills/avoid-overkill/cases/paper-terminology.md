# Paper terminology and translation

**Situation:** A concept has related names across subfields but no settled Chinese translation.

**Bad move:** Translate each English word literally, coin a polished label, alternate several synonyms, and call the label a standard academic term.

**General response:** Identify the exact concept, reuse the field's canonical English term, and give a natural Chinese explanation at first mention. If the paper introduces a working label, say so and use it consistently.

**Rule:** Translate the concept in context, not the surface words. Natural target-language syntax matters more than preserving source-language order.
